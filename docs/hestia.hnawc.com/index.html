Core features

Convenience starts with the basics.

GameBanana browser
01

Browse. Pick. Install.

Deep GameBanana API integration lets you browse and download mods directly in the app.

02

Automatic checking and updates

Hestia checks managed mods for available updates and updates them automatically while respecting local changes.

03

Drag & Drop Import

Drop in a mod, folder, or batch of archives then track their installation in one place.

04

Organize it your way.

Create categories that fit your setup, arrange them freely, and assign them to mods individually or in bulk.

And that’s just the start

There’s plenty more.

Read mod titles, descriptions, and metadata in your preferred language, automatically or on demand.

Follow downloads and installations as they happen, then cancel, retry, or resume them when needed.

Add custom notes, images, and metadata to any mod. Especially useful for unlinked or private mods.

Hestia finds supported games and mod folders automatically, with a deeper scan available when the usual locations come up empty.

Search, sort, filter, and group your library so the right mod is always easy to reach.

Choose your fonts and library preferences, configure a proxy, and keep frequently used external tools close at hand.
